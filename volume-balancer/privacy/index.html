中文

用途与访问的数据

Volume Balancer 在本机调节网页音视频音量和强弱变化。扩展在获准访问的 HTTP/HTTPS 网页和框架中识别媒体元素、播放及静音状态，并访问网页标题、地址、标签页及框架/文档标识，以显示和管理网页声音。自动处理在可用时读取播放器的实时音频副本。用户唤起扩展或选择整页接管后，可捕获所选标签页的实时音频，可能包含其中全部正在播放的声音；视频关闭，不请求麦克风或摄像头。

本机存储与保留

统一音量、自动/均衡开关及峰值上限保存在扩展的 local 存储，不使用同步存储。逐页音量/停止状态、捕获错误、媒体框架数量及文档标识保存在 session 存储；缺少文档标识时可能使用框架地址。关闭对应标签页时清理相关条目；浏览器重启、扩展重载或更新可能清空会话数据。实时音频采样、网页标题和电平指标用于运行及显示。扩展不录制或保存音频文件，不建立浏览历史数据库。

传输、共享及其他服务

声音处理在本机完成。扩展没有开发者音频服务器、遥测或广告统计 SDK，不主动向开发者或第三方传送上述数据，不出售数据，也不将其用于声音控制以外的广告用途。上游与参考项目的外部链接仅在用户点击时打开，目标网站另有隐私政策。浏览器、操作系统、网页及媒体服务自身的缓存、日志、备份或网络行为不属于扩展的这些保证。

查看、控制和删除

可在扩展面板查看当前设置与媒体状态、逐页静音或停止，关闭自动处理，或在 Edge 扩展管理中限制站点访问、禁用及卸载扩展。关闭自动模式不会停止已有整页接管，请另用停止整页或停止全部接管。停止后网页原声可能更响；声音未恢复时可刷新网页后重新播放。持久设置保留到被修改或由 Edge 清除扩展存储；卸载时存储清理由 Edge 管理。更换扩展 ID 不保证迁移设置。开发者未接收上述本机数据，不能从服务器检索或删除这些数据。如有隐私疑问，请使用上方真实联系渠道。

政策变更

若扩展的数据处理发生变化，应更新本页与生效日期，并同步商店披露。

English

Purpose and data accessed

Volume Balancer controls web audio/video volume and loudness changes on the device. On permitted HTTP/HTTPS pages and frames it identifies media elements, playback and mute states, and accesses page titles, addresses, tab and frame/document identifiers to display and manage page audio. Automatic processing reads a live copy of media audio when available. After the user invokes the extension or selects tab capture, it can capture live audio from the selected tab, potentially including all playing sounds in that tab. Video is disabled; microphone and camera access are not requested.

On-device storage and retention

Global volume, automatic/leveling switches and peak limits persist in extension local storage, without sync storage. Per-tab volume/stop controls, capture errors, media-frame counts and document identifiers use session storage; frame addresses may be used when document identifiers are unavailable. Related entries are cleared when a tab closes; browser restart, extension reload or update may clear session data. Live samples, page titles and level metrics are used during operation and display. The extension does not record or save audio files or maintain a browsing-history database.

Transmission, sharing and other services

Audio processing takes place on the device. The extension has no developer audio server, telemetry or advertising analytics SDK. It does not proactively transmit the above data to the developer or third parties, sell the data or use them for advertising unrelated to audio control. External upstream/reference links open only on user click and are subject to the destination website’s policies. Browser, operating-system, website and media-service caching, logging, backup and network behavior are outside these extension guarantees.

Access, control and deletion

View current settings and media status in the panel, mute or stop pages, turn off automatic processing, or restrict site access, disable or uninstall the extension in Edge. Turning off automatic mode does not stop existing tab captures; stop them separately or use Stop all. Original page audio may be louder after stopping. If audio does not recover, refresh the page and play again. Persistent preferences remain until changed or Edge clears extension storage; Edge manages removal during uninstall. Settings are not guaranteed to migrate to another extension ID. The developer does not receive these on-device data and cannot retrieve or delete them from a server. For privacy questions, use the real contact channel above.
